A couple of years ago, I casually wondered whether there'd be any chance of tellin' some tales in the Rebel Tent at Beautiful Days. When the call came out this year, I popped my head up again, and this time, Glenn said yes...:-)

![](/images/bdays_2026_rebel_tent_lineup.jpg)

Sign on was at a non-descript portakabin in "Purple" parking...

![](/images/bdays_2026_sign_on.jpg)

As we didn't get there till the Friday morning, Artist camping was filled up, but we got a space in an extra area marked out in the staff camping field, not far from the catering tent, which had free tea and coffee, but required tokens I didn't have for the food... *(As at Wickham, we could have paid an affordable amount — under a tenner — for a meal as well.)*

Most folk were in vans, and only the minority of us were in tents, which was another reason we were sent to camp where we were: that field had toilets...

... and showers... And if you ever wonder where the water goes? Into a pit...

![](/images/bdays_crew_camping_shower_pit.jpg)

Just outside the camping area, a couple of shuttle buses ran throughout the day, one via the public roads to Escot House and the Big Top area, the other to "backstage" (main stage)...

![](/images/bdays_2026_behind_mainstage.jpg)

...where there was a bar, and toilets...

Our wristbands gave us ready access through some of the gates, not least the gate alongside the main stage to the backstage bar area and the shuttle bus stop back up the hill to the camping area...

![](/images/bdays_2026_wristband.jpg)

...but not everywhere...

![](/images/bdays_2026_not_everywhere.jpg)

As I was on on the Friday...

![](/images/bdays_2026_monty_banner.jpg)

... we made out way to the Rebel Tent as soon as we'd pitched...

![](/images/bdays_2026_rebel_tent.jpg)

As in previous years, boards outside listed the line up...

![](/images/bdays_2026_rebel_tent_boards.jpg)

...including yours truly:-)

![](/images/bdays_2026_rebel_tent_protest_days_board.jpg)

The sent went well, tales of the Luddites, and I roamed the stage with a handheld mic. Some interesting chats afterwards, and a couple of copies of Protest Tales booklets sold.

The next day, a Rebel Tent merch tent appeared...

![](/images/bdays_2026_rebel_tent_merch.jpg)

...and graciously stocked some of my wares...

![](/images/bdays_2026_rebel_tent_protest_tales_merch.jpg)

On the saturday, Glenn had arranged for me to do a slot in the Majikal Youth teen tent. "Don't worry," he said, "you'll be fine," he said....

Erm....

As an adult free area, aside from the youth workers, I had to be escorted in...

Err....

...and they were lovely... Slouched around, not knowing what to expect, they listened, they engaged, and I told them some tales. One of the Luddite ones, of young men, aged nineteen to twenty-six, attacking mills and being hanged for their trouble. And of Nasreddin, the wise fool. And we could have gone on, but there was another workshop due to start.

Fabulous... And as I left, a confirmation that it had gone pretty well — "we didn't even have to tell them to clap at the end..."

So another incredible weekend, and another great opportunity to experience the production side of a festival. I've been really fortunate to have been presented with so many great experiences this summer, and it's given me a tiny insight into, and huge taste for, festival gigging.

I'm even more convinced there is scope for bringing story into predominantly music festivals as a credible performance thing, so it's up to me now to try to make more of that...
