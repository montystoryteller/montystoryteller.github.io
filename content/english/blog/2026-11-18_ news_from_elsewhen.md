I increasingly find myself digging into the newspaper archives chasing down a hint of an old news story, and several hours or days later returning with several thousand words of freshly transcribed news articles relating to a story that was perhaps common knowledge, if only for a time, not only locally, but nationally.

And then the process of reading and forgetting begins, as I try to come to a way of telling the story of an event, much as it might have been told at the time based on what people had heard of the story, from newspapers, and gossip, and half remembered facts.

And as I try to put sets of tales together, I've also noticed I've started trying to complement those old historical tales with traditional tales that somehow complement them.

And I wonder if that is the form I coming to, set wise, bringing news from *elsewhen*, if not elsewhere...
