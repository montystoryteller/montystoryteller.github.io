The plan was simple. Trek up to Huddersfield on the Wednesday, pop in to the Irish session in the Grove on the Thursday, then down to Wroot for Gig In A Field on the Friday, and *Tales After Dark* session that I'd late pitched to Neil (Sanderson) and backed up with a ["did an open mic at Ey Up"](/blog/2026-05-11_open_mic_eyupmiduck/).

![](/images/giaf_2026_tales_after_dark.jpg)

Only it didn't quite go like that...

![](car_oops.jpg)

Oops...

NFU Mutual were really good, though, got me a taxi up to Huddersfield and arranged a hire car, so I was able to make it down to Wroot — after a most enjoyable evening at the Irish Session in the The Grove the night before — on the Friday as planned.

Once again, I got to avail myself of production parking, this time behind the main stage, where I was also able to pitch a tent.

![](/images/giaf_2026_main_stage.jpg)

*(I would have gone into the main camping area, but the toilets closer backstage!)*

![](/images/giaf_2026_wristband.jpg)

The set-up was a single stage, with a small chill-out tent where I;d be doing my *Tales After Dark*.

![](/images/giaf_2026_the_stage_billing.jpg)

As this was just a try out thing, my payment was a handful of beer tokens...

![](/images/giaf_2026_drinks_vouchers.jpg)

...and access to the backstage hospitality tent...

![](/images/giaf_2026_hospitality.jpg)

That said, the honesty shop out front was also well stocked, and included a kettle and tea bags if anyone wanted a cup of tea that way, though as with 'Ey Up, the food trucks were excellent once again.

![](/images/giaf_2026_honesty_shop.jpg)

As well as the honesty shop, there was also a clothing exchange...

![](/images/giaf_2026_clothing_exchange.jpg)

Neil, Amber, Julie and the whole Wroot Rocks team certainly know how to make a festival work as community:-)

![](/images/giaf_2026_mainstage2.jpg)

The *Tales After Dark* set went well — we'd agreed a shortish slot so th eten could be freed up for musicians, but I kept asking, and no musicians seemed to to appear, so I just carried on tellin' tales...

Next morning, having a keep of hospitality tea...

![](/images/giaf_2026_backstage_kettle.jpg)

... I overhead Julie wondering if Joe Solo, who was due to open, could move up a slot to fill a no-show. A no-show? So an empty stage, early doors? Maybe I could help???

Erm....

![](/images/giaf_2026_the_stage.jpg)

My first main stage:-) And opening it too:-)

![](/images/giaf_2026_monty_on_stage.jpg)

And as a reward...

![](/images/giaf_2026_food_voucher.jpg)

I played that stage:-)

![](/images/giaf_2026_arena.jpg)

A truly brilliant weekend, notwithstanding the written off car along the way... And a huge thanks to Julie for trusting me with the main stage...
