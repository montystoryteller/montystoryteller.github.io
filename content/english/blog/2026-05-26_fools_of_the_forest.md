I ummed, and ahhed, and changed my mind several times over the course of the day about what to tell, and how to tell it, but it all came together in the end...

The original premise had been to shape the set around several tales of cunning folk from the Forest, but the tales I had seemed a bit thin until I took the decision to contrast several "historical" tales of cunning folk and fools who visited them with *folk* tales of cunning folk and the fools who visited *them*.

*I'm actually retro-posting this post — as I write it, we're in mid-September, 2026 — and I `think` the set was something like this...*

- (Wise folk in story) Pottle of Brains
- Pig in the forest.
- Lost horses in the forest
- Folk tale - hare
- Hare Ross tale
- Leaves that hung
- (interval)
- Hudden, Dudden, Donal o'Leary
- Bowl love most
