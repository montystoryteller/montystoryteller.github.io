My first (outdoor) festival as a billed performer, and a great way to start the summer...

We were just back from a set of tales at the Crown & Sceptre in Ross, and five days at Bearded Theory, where I have a hazy recollection of telling around the fire, and in the Magic Teapot, but it was all a bit alco-pops and I won't do that again...

Then it was back to the Island, the Three Rivers... and my first accreditation sign on...

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_accreditation.jpg)

I went in early to get a feel for the layout of the site, and to check the stages out...

First up would be the Woodland Stage...

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_arrow_woodland.jpg)

![](/images/three_rivers_woodland_stage_setup.jpg)

Then the Community Stage...

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_arrow_community_stage.jpg)

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_community_stage_setup.jpg)

Being new to having performer accreditation, I wasn't really sure about what the perks and bonuses might be...

It was pretty obvious what drink tokens were for...

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_tokens.jpg)

...but I wasn't really sure about access. I obviously had access to the Woodland and Community Stage, but I wasn't sure if there was any hospitality in the main stage backstage area, and didn't try going there, but as I'm a little more confident about how things work now, I know I just need to get a glimpse of the access sheet and that'll tell if I can can go that way or not...

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_wristband.jpg)

My first set, with Sophie, was lunchtime Sunday, on the Woodland Stage.

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_woodland_takeover.jpg)

We got in early, but I'd worked through a lot of my nerves the day before, getting up to tell a quick tale on the same stage in an open mic session. *This was absolutely the RIGHT thing to do... It took away a lot of the uncertainty about how to actually get onto the stage, prepped the feeling of nerves just in advance of walking up onto the stage, and gave me a sense of what it felt like to be on that stage. And how to set myself up for using the mic...*

And then we were on... and had a blast...

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_w_sophie.jpg)

And then time for a break, a quick run through of one of the tales I was doing with Sue, (in retrospect, for me, we should have gone through both, just to help me rehearse the possible paths I could take, and spot the bits I might forget...) before taking to the Community Stage...

![](/images/community_stage_w_anywhens_three_rivers_2026.jpg)

... for what would turn out to be the last outing of Monty + The AnyWhens...

...and the first and last time to have our names as a troupe on a t-shirt or hoodie...

![](/images/three_rivers_2026_hoodie.jpg)
