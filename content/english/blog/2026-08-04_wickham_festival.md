Back at the start of the year, when I'd been monitoring various pages looking for a way to pitch in to [Wickham Festival](https://www.wickhamfestival.co.uk/), I noticed that the [Magic Teapot](https://www.magicteapot.co.uk/) folk were taking *three* Teapots to the festival: the middle sized session Teapot, a large VIP area Teapot, and a tiny storytelling Teapot.

I'd guested with resident teller [*Travelling Talesman*](https://thetravellingtalesman.co.uk/) Cliff Eastabrook last year, and wondered if there might be an opportunity to do the same. Due to "circs", Cliff was unable to cover Wickham this year, so I wondered if I could help out taking on a session or two...

Messages were exchanged, emails too, and it looked like it was on, or maybe not, and maybe a few sessions, but I had "ideas", (not wanting to tread on Cliff's toes, trying to offer a complement rather than a strict replacement), and even up to the start of the festival itself, or at least, the Friday, when we arrived, it wasn't clear what I might do...

The Tiny Teapot was pitched, at the bottom right corner of the field, opposite the *Underground Overground* stage I'd hoped to pitch a story'n'band thing into, with no sign of it being used for anything...

... so I thought I'd co-opt it anyway, went and found some paper and pens, and started making a sign.

One of Cliff's loyal followers wandered by, asked what I was doing. Sent a message to Cliff, and a message to Peter (Chegwyn, whose festival it is)... Joe, from the Magic Teapot, wandered by, asked what I was doing. Phoned Peter... who came down... and took me off to get a wristband...

![](/images/wickham_2026_wristband.jpg)

It was agreed I'd do what sessions I could, given we were there primarily as punters (Fericious Dog, Black Water County...), and Peter took me off to meet the folks in the Production Office behind the main stage to get some printed schedule sheets to replace my hand-made ones.

![](/images/wickham_2026_tiny_teapot.jpg)

I also managed to get a couple of meal tickets...

![](/images/wickham_2026_meal_ticket.jpg)

...for use in the [Iechyd da Catering](https://www.facebook.com/people/Iechyd-da-catering/100050547267013/) tent...

![](/images/wickham_2026_catering_menu_1.jpg)

![](/images/wickham_2026_catering_menu_2.jpg)

...with its panoramic backstage view...

![](/images/wickham_2026_from_catering_tent.jpg)

It was early afternoon by now, but still plenty of time for a notice announcing a 40 min *"Tales For All Ages"* slot at half-three to do its job (a couple of sandwich boards outside would have been even better), as well as a poster announcing a *"Tales After Dark"* late show after 11 when the main stage had shut down. As expected, the afternoon show was kids...

...and great fun it was too... Having learned a lot from Trowbridge, it was easy to get into the swing of it, not really telling the tales any differently to how I normally tell them, but being differently playful with them (though definitely *not* affecting any voices, and *not* wearing anything other than a customary t-shirt).

Tales told, I went to pick up my printing from the production office...

![](/images/wickham_2026_teapot_after_dark_notice.jpg)

Then a quick visit back to the tent before heading in again, making use of one of the biggest perks of the AAA wristband — the ability to take a short cut into the site through an otherwise closed gate...

![](/images/wickham_2026_festival_sunset.jpg)

At which point, I realised that it would be dark in the Tiny Teapot...

Another trip up to the Production Office... "It'll be dark", I said, "for the tales after dark", I said, "there's no lights in the Tiny Teapot." Production Offices, it seems, being Production Offices set up to build small towns and run large events in off-grid fields, take these things in their stride. An electrician was found, and lo, there was light.

![](/images/wickham_2026_tiny_teatent_lights.jpg)

For the late show, it was obvious who had turned up: Cliff's loyal following. This would be the real test... But I had guested the year before, so I wasn't a totally alien presence. I explained Cliff was unable to make, explained how as I was at the festival anywhere, I was happy to provide some stories, at least, and we began...

And then it went dark. The lights were motion activated, but on a timer... The set changed slightly, summoning gestures from the audience every so often (when I remembered), hilarity  and darkness, when I didn't.

The next morning — with a single *"Tales For All Ages"* show planned for half three — we did a quick run back to Portsmouth Harbourm, where I hopped on a cat back to Ryde Pier Head, picked up a box of merch from our next door neighbours, who'd been instructed what to find, and where — and made same boat back.

![](/images/wickham_2026_merch.jpg)

Another afternoon set, and a lot of repeat visits from the day before, which was really encouraging, then backstage for a free cup of tea in the catering tent. (I quickly realised that, along with the ability to take short cuts, this was another *major perk*!)...

![](/images/wickham_2026_coffee_tea.jpg)

...as was the oppotunity to wander round the production area of the main stage and the second tent...

![](/images/wickham_2026_backstage.jpg)

...then ready for the evening show. And a quick check of the lights. Which didn't work...

"Me again," in the Production Office. But after ten, it seems, on a Saturday night, it seems, when the main band is on and all is pretty much done for the day, the A-team, who can just do things, are maybe not staffing the office... I ask a security guard if he happens to know where I might find an electrician. He points to a chap walking by..."He's one..."

Sorted.

Tripped switch...

...then timed out.

One of the lights refuses to come back on... But that's okay... We've all found a rhythm now...

![](/images/wickham_2026_tellin.jpg)

Sunday. I opt to do an early *Tales for Ages*, at half one, a mid-afternoon, at half-three, an early evening historical set (*John the Painter*, a local-ish story), and a final late.

Five minutes into the early afternoon show, the competition starts up, right outside...

![](/images/wickham_2026_punch_judy.jpg)

I finish the tale quickly, and we bring that set to an end.

A negotiation with the Punch and Judy crew — my next set has been "advertised" — and they agree to put their back so we don't clash again...

...then on to the evening set, and the late set, and we're done...

![](/images/wickham_2026_sunset2.jpg)

...except for a bit of clearing up, taking the posters down, and leaving the Tiny Teapot as we found it...

All in all, an incredible experience... Thanks to Peter Chegwyn for taking me on trust, and hopefully Cliff will be back next year... though obviously, given the chance, I've also love to be able to fill a slot or two...
