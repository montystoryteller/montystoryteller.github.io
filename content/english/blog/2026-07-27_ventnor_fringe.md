Ventnor Fringe calls earlier each year — I think applications opened up around about November last year (2025) for 2026, but I held off for some time as I went round in circles for what show to do as a solo show, and (event then) not overly comfortable with the *Faery Tale Blues* show that was proposed with The AnyWhens.

Holly was likely doing a set of Island Tales (originally a smuggling tales set, I think, although it became a little wider in scope when she finally pitched it as "The Fabled Isle") and I was originally thinking of doing *Newport nursery of Vice*, thinking that for an afternoon slot, historical tales might work best and guarantee a good audience. But that didn't feel right, so in the end I opted for *Solent Tales*, eventually telling *A Tragic Affair*, *The Ghost that Drove out the Ghost*, and fragments of David Tyrie, and the Royal George. I'd also prepped *The Solent Mutiny*, but the hour flew by too quickly to include that.

![](/images/flyers_gallery/2026_07_22_monty_solent.png)

I had hoped to have merch, too, but Royal Mail really seem to struggle with delivering things from KDPs print-on-demand warehoue, and the package was lost in transit.

Though there was also unease in the camp around *Faery Tale Blues*, that also went into the programme...

...but a few short weeks before the Fringe was due to start, and after a long period of none of us working, together, at least, on developing the show, "narrative differences" meant we had to change plans...

Sue and I were still keen to do something, and Sophie was also available for one of the dates, so we had a rethink...

![](/images/flyers_gallery/2026_07_23_faery_tale_blues_monty_sue_vfringe_26.png)

For the Thursday, we had *Twa Sisters*, *True Thomas*, *Fairy Washerwoman*, *Raggle Taggle* and *Fairy Ointment*. There was a lovely bounce of dialogue planned between myself and Sue for the end of *True Thomas*, which I completely messed up, missed my cue, and spoke Sue's part, so on the second day (where we dropped *Washerwoman* and *Raggle Taggle*, and brought in *Fisherman's Wife*, and a song I forget the name of into *Fairy Ointment*), Sue pranked me back, casting doubt into my telling of *Twa Sisters* — "are you sure it was...?" — and making the set even more playful than it already was.
