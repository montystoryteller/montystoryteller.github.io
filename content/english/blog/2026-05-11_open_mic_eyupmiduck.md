Ever since I came across the Wroot Rocks crew, and the homegrown events they promote just outside Doncaster, I wanted to try to get to them. The line ups are full of the artists I think of as being in the [New Troubadours](htttps://newtroubadours.org) vein, which is to say, the sort of artist where a combination with a storyteller in support might possibly work out, as well as the sorts of band we go to see at Bearded and BDays, and wheresoever, whensoever, we get the chance. So as "research" I got myself a very affordable ticket to 'Ey Up Mi Duck, 2026.

![](/images/eyupmiduck_2026.jpg)

I'd booked a room in a nearby pub, so wasn't doing the camping thing (too early in the year for that!). The site was all in a single field, with the two main stages at either end of a single large tent. As soon as one band went off, there was a five minute sound check at the other end, and the next band started up. There was also a second, open mic, tent that ran all weekend, a local brewery beer tent, and some excellent food vans. And an Sunday evening tea time finish which meant I could get a boat back home the same night.

![](eyupmiduck_2026_tents.jpg)

The open mic tent, run by Pete (Drake) had a pretty solid schedule. I blagged a short slot on the Saturday...

![](eyupmiduck_2026_openmic_sat.jpg)

...and when that seemed to go okay, grabbed another for the Sunday...

![](/images/eyupmiduck_2026_openmic_sun.jpg)

Some great comments after — and folk I would see and recognise as new friends at festivals later in the year — Ey Up Mi Duck was everything I thought it might be, and more. A really friendly and welcoming vibe, good music, good beer, great food, and a chance to tell a couple of tales...

...and maybe a chance to pitch a late night / after hours storytelling session in the open mic tent for next year..?
