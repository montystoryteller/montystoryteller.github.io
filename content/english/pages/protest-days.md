### Tales told as part of Protest Days, Rebellion Nights

Tales retold from troubled times, when action was taken directly and the consequences could be considerable.

Tales include:

- Land protest (e.g. Kett's Rebellion, Wye Forest Levellers)
- Machine Breaking (Luddites)
- Marches (Blanketeers)
- Rebellion (Pentrich Revolution, Folly Hall Affair)
- Mutiny (Solent mutiny)
- Spies (Oliver the Spy, David Tyrie)
- Subversives (John the Painter)
